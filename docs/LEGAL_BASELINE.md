# Legal baseline

Baseline reviewed: 2026-08-25.

Primary EU source:
- Council Directive 2006/112/EC, consolidated 14 April 2025.
- EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2006/112/2025-04-14

Key implemented topics:
- Articles 31 and 32 — place of supply.
- Article 36a — chain-transaction transport attribution.
- Articles 40, 41 and 42 — intra-Community acquisition and safety-net rule.
- Article 138(1)/(1a) — intra-Community exemption including VAT-ID and recapitulative-statement conditions.
- Articles 141 and 197 — triangular simplification.
- T-646/24 — four-party physical delivery context and fraud/abuse limitation.
- C-247/21 Luxury Trust — triangular invoice reverse-charge wording.

National reverse-charge coverage is intentionally narrower than EU coverage. Only a verified subset is active; other countries require explicit legal review.
